# Community Code of Conduct

Altruforge welcomes people with different backgrounds, experience levels, identities, and ways of contributing.

## Expected behavior

Be considerate and specific. Critique proposals and code with evidence, acknowledge uncertainty, and help others understand decisions. Respect personal boundaries and the time of maintainers and contributors.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory attacks, sexual harassment, doxxing, impersonation, and repeated disruptive behavior are not acceptable. Do not pressure people to disclose private information or participate outside their chosen boundaries.

These expectations apply to project issues, pull requests, reviews, and other spaces represented as part of Altruforge.

## Reporting and response

For a project conduct concern, open a minimal issue titled **Request for a private conduct contact**, without naming affected people or posting sensitive details. A maintainer will establish a suitable channel before collecting a report. A dedicated confidential project contact has not yet been published; do not assume a public issue is private.

For content that violates GitHub's own policies, use GitHub's built-in reporting controls or [GitHub Support](https://support.github.com/).

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, close disruptive discussions, or restrict participation. They should explain decisions where doing so does not expose private information. A person affected by a moderation decision may request reconsideration without repeating sensitive details publicly.

The initial responsible maintainer is [Lephiziel](https://github.com/Lephiziel). A report involving the maintainer can also be directed to GitHub for platform-policy violations.
