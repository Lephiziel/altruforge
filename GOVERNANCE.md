# Governance

## Ownership and decisions

Altruforge is initially maintained by [Lephiziel](https://github.com/Lephiziel). The maintainer decides project direction, merges, release readiness, moderation, and access changes.

Contributors can propose improvements through issues and pull requests. A substantial change to the specification or trust boundary should include an ADR with alternatives, consequences, and evidence.

## Decision records

An accepted ADR records a project decision. An ADR marked proposed or evidence-gated must not be presented as a verified implementation. Replacement decisions supersede old records explicitly.

Before the first stable specification, breaking draft changes are possible but must update prose, schemas, examples, and consumers together. Stable compatibility rules are described in [spec/README.md](spec/README.md).

## Access and releases

Repository access is granted explicitly. Contribution volume, AI usage, or donated resources do not automatically grant collaborator privileges or merge rights.

A release needs the evidence in [release readiness](docs/release-readiness.md). The maintainer controls publication. The product MVP itself does not automatically merge contributed PRs.

## Growing the team

When recurring maintainers join, document their responsibilities, decision procedure, and a reporting contact before claiming a broader governance structure.

## Commercial use

The project uses Apache-2.0. Commercial services and closed derivatives are permitted under its terms. Naming and compatibility claims must not imply endorsement by Altruforge or a model provider.
